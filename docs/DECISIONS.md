# Chore Wars — Decision & Risk Log

Lightweight record of product and technical decisions—especially where human product intuition intentionally overrides an evidenced technical concern—so we always remember **why** a call was made, **what risk** we accepted, and **how we'll mitigate it**.

## Template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short Decision Title
- **Decision:** What we decided to do.
- **Why (Rationale):** The human/product/UX reasoning behind the choice.
- **Identified Risks:** Concrete technical, UX, or business risks raised during discussion.
- **Mitigations / Revisit Trigger:** Safeguards in place today and when we'll re-evaluate.
